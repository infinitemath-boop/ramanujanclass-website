How we teach

Concepts first, then exam-level problems, then a test

Each class builds a topic from its definitions, works through problems at CSIR NET and GATE level, and is followed by a test the same night.

  1. Live concept class

    Topics are built up from first principles on the board, with the reasoning written out in full.

  2. Recorded for revision

    Every class is recorded and appears in the app within the day, cropped to the board so the writing is easy to read.

  3. Daily test

    A 40-minute test after each class: MCQs with negative marking and CSIR NET style MSQs, all with worked solutions.

  4. Weekly test & rank list

    A timed weekly paper across the week's classes, with a batch rank list by marks and time.

The class app

Your classes, tests and books in one place

Students sign in at app.ramanujanclass.org with the email ID they registered with. It works in any phone or computer browser and can be added to the home screen like an app.

  • Recorded lectures, day by day, with a board view that fills the screen with the writing.
  • Daily and weekly tests in the real exam pattern, shuffled on every attempt, with full solutions.
  • Batch rank list for every weekly test.
  • My progress shows your weak areas, and Revise now brings back the questions you got wrong until you master them.
  • Notes, practice questions and a book library with soft copies of the standard texts.

About us

A mathematics class in Namakkal

Ramanujan Class is run by an IITian who holds an M.Sc. from IIT Madras and has qualified CSIR NET, GATE, IIT JAM and TN SET, the same exams the class prepares you for. Course mentor: Karthika Jothimani.

The class prepares students for CSIR NET, GATE Mathematics, IIT JAM, TN SET and PG TRB, and runs IIT Foundation and Olympiad coaching for school students.

Classes follow each exam's syllabus subject by subject, and every topic ends with a timed test in the exam's own pattern. Tests, notes and announcements are also shared on the Infinite Mathematics blog.

The name behind the class

  1. 1887

    Srinivasa Ramanujan is born in Erode on 22 December. His family deity is Namagiri Thayar of Namakkal.

  2. 1913

    He writes to G. H. Hardy at Cambridge, enclosing pages of original theorems.

  3. 1918

    He is elected Fellow of the Royal Society, one of the youngest in its history.

  4. 1920

    He dies at 32 in Kumbakonam, leaving notebooks still studied today.

  5. 22 December

    India celebrates his birthday as National Mathematics Day.
